# Manipulation and the rig in `ftgsxr-app`

How `ftgsxr_app::manipulate` meets picking, selection, grabs and the pose store (E10, E11, §1.5 Poses, §1.7 Selection and manipulation, §1.8 Rays, selection and grabbing, D-11, D-42, D-43), and how `ftgsxr_app::rig` meets the player rig, the content group, locomotion and the desktop camera (E12, §1.7 Cameras, §1.8 Locomotion, §1.14 Frames, D-17, D-44). The spec items are those, §1.8 Session and spaces for the recenter and §2.1's entries for both modules; this doc explains them and never overrides them. Built in M-4 (#282, #283); the hover per frame, the headset's automatic fit, the recenter and the teleport in M-5 (#453, #455).

## `manipulate`

A private `ManipulatePlugin`, after `PlaybackPlugin`. One system reads the frame's intents in order and keeps each hand's last ray and pose, so a `Select` picks from that hand's ray at its point in the order (Q-182). On the headset a hand whose ray is on a panel quad writes no `Pointer`, `Select` or grab that frame (`ftgsxr-xr/xr_input.md`), so a pinch on the panel never picks the instance behind it (Q-263).

- **Hover and pick.** `Hovered` is recomputed each frame from that frame's `Pointer` intents: after the intents, the last ray of each hand that wrote one is picked over every instance with a box, hidden ones included, with `pick_instance`, and a hand without a `Pointer` that frame hovers nothing (Q-263, rewording Q-182's "last ray", which stays the rule within a frame for `Select`); an instance hit by two hands carries the nearer hit (Q-182). The desktop's adapter writes the mouse's `Pointer` every frame the cursor is over the viewport.
- **Grabs.** One- and two-hand grabs follow E10 and E11. Each hand's grab point is the point of the instance where its ray took hold (E10's hit, the ray's origin from inside the box), kept in the instance's frame; a two-hand grab hands `TwoHandGrab` the two grab points, each fixed in its hand's frame from the grab's start, or the hands' aim positions when the points start closer than 5 cm (spec revision 34). Push and pull move along the hand's ray in proportion to the hit distance at the grab's start; any pose change outside the grab (push and pull, nudge, turn, fit, reset, snap to floor) begins the grab again from the new pose, a two-hand grab with each grab point at the same place on the instance (Q-183). `Turn` rotates about stage +y through the instance's origin; `Nudge` moves in stage space (Q-184).
- **Fit and presets.** When `LoadedModel` appears without a saved pose, the up-axis preset comes from the first `ftgs-scene` manifest beside a local model, else Y-up, and a checkpoint's spatial scale gives L_f (Q-181). The fit's head is the active camera of the lowest order that renders to a window or to the headset's views, never to an image, the identity pose with none (Q-180, Q-310): `ui`'s panel cameras (order −1, `ui.md`) render to images, so under the `Headset` marker the head is an eye. One crate-private `head_pose` serves the fit, the automatic fit, the gizmo's size, locomotion, the teleport and the recenter below, and `capture`'s fallback prefers such a camera, taking an image-target one only when no other is active (Q-212's camera that renders only to an image). The fit and snap to floor take the fit box, `SplatBounds.content` or `.aabb` as `ManipulateSettings.fit_to_content` says, which `persist` keeps equal to `[general] fit_to` (Q-371); every other reader (picking, the drawn boxes, the gizmo, orbit, recenter) takes `SplatBounds.aabb`, B\_m, trimmed while a trim is on (E17, `ftgsxr-render/trim.md`). Only the automatic fit at load reads the asset's untrimmed boxes (Q-374). The Scene tab's "Fit to" toggle, beside each instance's Fit, flips that one global value.
- **The headset's automatic fit** (§1.5 Poses, D-11, Q-284). With the `Headset` marker (Q-266), when `LoadedModel` appears on an instance without a saved pose and its fit box's longest side lies outside §1.5's range (the fit box is E10's content box B\_c under `ManipulateSettings.fit_to_content`, the default, else B\_m; spec revision 44, Q-371), the instance takes `fit_pose` with its hints' preset and L\_fit (Q-181) and the fit's head (Q-180), written relative to the content group (Q-247). Every load of a model without a saved pose is a first load; a reload keeps its `LoadedModel` (Q-238) and is not fitted again; a scene's pose, applied after the instance appears, replaces the fit. `play --xr` has no marker and no automatic fit.
- **Gizmo.** The desktop's gizmo handles sit at the posed box's center along the stage axes, sized from the viewer's distance; only the mouse uses them, and a handle hit on `Select` starts a drag instead of a pick (Q-186). The hovered and selected boxes and the gizmo are drawn with Bevy's gizmo lines (§1.4 Selection and debug views): the hovered box light grey and the selected one orange, or under `[display] high_contrast` yellow and cyan, a crate-private `HighContrast` that `persist` sets from the settings (Q-278, Q-312).
- **Trim handles.** E18's handles live in the private `manipulate/trim_handles.rs` (their size, the ray's hit, the drag point, the merge of two hands' drags), and `run_intents` keeps the drags, one per hand, beside the grabs: `Select` (and on a hand `GrabBegin`) tries the selected instance's handles before the pick or the grab, a dragging hand ignores its other actions, and an instance with a dragged handle refuses grabs while a grabbed one hides its handles. At the end of each frame the drags whose instance was deselected, whose trim mode changed or whose mouse left the gizmo's trim mode end, the rest recompute their drag points from their hands' last rays, and each dragged instance's trim is written as `SetTrim` would (Q-374, Q-379). When a drag of an instance starts or ends, its drags re-anchor first: each takes as t₀ the trim they last sent it, else its trim, and its last drag point as p₀, so the trim does not jump and a release keeps the other hand's edits (Q-379, #655). `TrimHandles` mirrors the hovered and dragged handles for `audio`'s sounds and the XR haptics, and `draw_trim_handles` draws the handles in a gizmo group of their own, on top (Q-379).
- **Frames.** An instance's `Transform` is its pose relative to its parent, `ContentGroup`; its stage pose is that pose followed by the group's transform (§1.14). Picking, hover, grabs (keep upright included, about stage +y, Q-248), push and pull, turn, nudge, fit, snap to floor, the gizmo and the drawn boxes all work on the stage pose, since the rays, the head and the floor are in stage space, and write the result back relative to the group through the group's inverse (§1.14). E11's scale limits bound the stage scale (Q-249).
- **Poses.** "Save pose" stores the pose relative to the content group, and "Reset pose" and a saved pose applied on load write it back as it is (Q-247). This module changes `Poses` in memory only; `persist` writes `poses.json` (Q-205).
- **Defaults.** `ManipulateSettings::default()` and an empty `Poses`; `AppPlugin` inserts each when absent, so `persist` or a caller may insert them first (Q-185).
- **Keep upright.** `ManipulateSettings.keep_upright` is the settings' `[xr] keep_upright`, which `persist` maps onto it both ways (Q-330): on, a two-hand grab turns by yaw only, and a one-hand grab's grab point follows the hand while the instance turns about it by the hand's yaw only (the twist about stage +y of the hand's turn); off, a two-hand grab takes E11's free rotation and a one-hand grab is rigid, P = H M (E11, spec revision 37, D-43). The main panel's one-hand grab is rigid whatever the switch (E11). The main panel's two-hand resize is `ftgsxr_xr::panel`'s and stays upright whatever the switch (Q-306).

## `rig`

A private `RigPlugin`, after `ManipulatePlugin`. `PlayerRig` and `ContentGroup` are spawned in every app; the desktop camera, a child of the rig, and the floor grid, an opaque unlit line mesh at y = 0, only where a primary window exists (Q-194).

- **Locomotion.** `apply_locomotion` is a pure function of the rig's and the head's stage poses and one intent: smooth movement in the head's yaw frame, smooth or snap turns about the vertical through the head (Q-195). `stick_response` is E12's response curve on a raw stick value, pure. On the headset the rig is the XR tracking space: `ftgsxr-xr`'s session binds the tracking root under it (`ftgsxr-xr/session.md`), so moving the rig moves the eyes and the hands.
- **Teleport** (§1.8 Locomotion, Q-282). `Intent::Teleport { target }` is applied in the frame's intent order with `Locomote` and `SnapTurn`: the rig's translation gains the horizontal step from the head to the target, its rotation, scale and height unchanged, the target's y not read. The head is the one those intents use, the fit's (Q-180, Q-310), carried with the rig within the frame, the rig itself without a camera. Nothing moves while `Locomotion.enabled` is false (passthrough mode, D-17); `Locomotion.teleport` does not gate it, since it selects the arc upstream (Q-269, `ftgsxr-xr/xr_input.md`). `apply_locomotion` keeps Q-195's contract.
- **Recenter** (§1.8 Session and spaces, Q-283). `Intent::Recenter` translates the content group horizontally, its rotation and scale kept so it stays yaw-only (Q-254), so that a point c stands §1.8's distance ahead of the head along its horizontal forward (Q-17) at the height c has: c is the selected instance's posed box center, else the mean of every loaded instance's (those with `LoadedModel`). The head is the fit's (Q-180, Q-310), and the distance is the fit's 1.5 m, one constant in `manipulate`. It works in passthrough mode too (D-17: the recenter brings the content to the user), and moves nothing without an instance. It runs after locomotion and before the view following.
- **The desktop camera.** A crate-private `CameraController` holds the camera mode, the orbit and nine bookmark slots; it reads `Intent::Camera`, with the units and limits of Q-196. `desktop_input` builds the intents against those units, and `persist` saves the mode, the orbit and the bookmarks (Q-206).
- **Jumps.** A crate-private `CameraJump` moves the fly camera to a training camera's pose and field of view (Q-219, `ui.md`).
